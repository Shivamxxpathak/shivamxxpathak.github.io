# SuperStore Data Analytics

**Category:** Data Analytics / Business Intelligence  
**Stack:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly, Excel, Jupyter/Google Colab

End-to-end retail analytics project focused on sales, profitability, discount impact, returns, product performance, regional analysis, trends, executive KPIs, and business recommendations.

**Key results:** $2.33M sales · $292.30K profit · 12.56% profit margin · 5,111 orders · 804 customers
